Prompt final utilizado:

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: landscape website background, wide 3:2 composition. Create a professional vintage illustrated enchanted bayou garden at night inspired by The Princess and the Frog: deep forest green and moss green lake, ivory lotus flowers clustered at lower corners, aged brass filigree accents, willow silhouettes and distant antique New Orleans garden pavilion. Very subtle golden fireflies and soft emerald moonlight. Painterly gouache on aged fine paper with elegant detailed botanical engraving. Center 65% must remain extremely dark, quiet and uncluttered for legible cream website content. Main details only edges and lower sides. Rich beautiful scene, not flat color, no photographs, no human figures, no text, no letters, no watermark, no simulated UI, no frames. Moody romantic tasteful antique colors; avoid bright yellow or saturated green. Wide high quality background.

As capturas do usuário foram usadas somente para diagnóstico. Não foram entradas desta geração.
